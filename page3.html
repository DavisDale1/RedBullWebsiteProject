01 // Where it started

From the couch
to the cockpit.

Watching the race was fun.
We wanted to feel the start.

Our idea started during a race-day watch party. Between cheering for Red Bull Racing and debating who would set the fastest lap, Ross, Dominic, and Davis asked a simple question: what if we could bring that excitement to all F1 fans?

That question became Oracle Red Bull Racing Simulator Experience: a place where first-time drivers and lifelong fans could climb into a racing simulator, learn the circuit, and challenge their friends. Inspired by the energy and teamwork of Formula 1, we built our experience around one goal: make every guest feel like part of the grid.

Before lights out

Good questions.
Quick answers.

Do I need racing experience?

No experience? No problem. Our Intro Experience includes a welcome briefing, cockpit setup, and guided driving so you can learn before chasing your fastest lap.

Can I race with my friends?

That's the idea! Our Team Grand Prix package is designed for up to four drivers on individual simulators, with practice, qualifying, a championship race, and a podium celebration. Explore the packages.

How long is a session?

Our packages range from 60 to 120 minutes. Those times include briefings, setup, coaching, and any scheduled breaks, so the full session is not continuous driving.

What should I bring to my session?

Wear comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes, and bring your competitive spirit. We provide the simulator equipment and guide you through your cockpit setup before the session starts.

Do I get a trophy if I win?

The winner earns a podium moment and serious bragging rights. The friendly rivalry is included.
