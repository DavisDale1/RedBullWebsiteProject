Racing packages

Four premium motorsport experiences featuring proposed high-end motion simulators, force-feedback controls, personal calibration, and professional-style race support. Session durations include briefings, breaks, and coaching—not continuous driving.

01 / INTRO EXPERIENCE

The Intro Experience

$399

60 MINUTES
A 15-minute welcome, safety briefing, and cockpit calibration followed by approximately 45 minutes of guided driving, practice laps, and a timed challenge. Designed for first-time drivers.

02 / SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE

The Signature Experience

$749

90 MINUTES
Experience a complete Grand Prix format: 20 minutes of practice, 15 minutes of qualifying, a 10-minute break, and a 45-minute race. A true race-weekend challenge.

How it works
03 / ELITE DRIVER

The Elite Driver Experience

$1,499

120 MINUTES
A professional-style training block combining approximately 60 minutes of driving with 60 minutes of off-rig telemetry review, expert coaching, and feedback between driving stints.

How it works
04 / PRIVATE GROUP

Team Grand Prix

$1,999

120 MINUTES · UP TO 4 DRIVERS
Four drivers compete on individual motion simulators: 15-minute fitting, 25-minute practice, 20-minute break and telemetry briefing, 15-minute qualifying, 35-minute championship race, and 10-minute podium celebration.
